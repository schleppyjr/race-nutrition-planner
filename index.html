    What the carb numbers look like

    Carb targets are per day and include drinks. Protein stays near 1.6 g/kg; fat and fiber come down in the last two days so the gut is light on race morning. Expect to gain 1 to 2 kg over the load: that is glycogen plus the water it stores, and it is the point.

    Guidelines follow mainstream endurance sports-nutrition ranges: 30 to 90 g carbs per hour depending on gut training, roughly 75% sweat replacement capped near 900 ml/hr, 300 to 1000 mg sodium per hour, caffeine 2 to 5 mg/kg.
